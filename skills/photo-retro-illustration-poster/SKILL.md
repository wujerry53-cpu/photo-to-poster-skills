# Photo × Retro Hand-Drawn Editorial Poster

## Objective

Transform each uploaded photograph into one independent 3:4 vertical design poster.

Never combine multiple uploaded photos into a collage.
If multiple photos are uploaded, create one separate poster per photo.

## 1. Inspect the photo

Identify:

- primary subject
- silhouette
- pose
- composition
- spatial relationships
- narrative relationships
- signature details
- dominant colors
- lighting and mood

## 2. Canvas

Create a vertical 3:4 canvas.

Strictly divide it horizontally:
- top = exactly 50%
- bottom = exactly 50%

## 3. Top half: original photography

Preserve:

- subject structure
- proportions
- pose
- perspective
- realistic texture
- natural lighting
- natural shadows
- original colors
- scene relationships

Only apply subtle editorial photographic grading.

If needed, extend only non-critical surroundings such as sky, ground, water, wall, foliage, or distant background.

Do not distort, redesign, or beautify the main subject.

## 4. Bottom half: retro editorial illustration

Reinterpret the scene rather than tracing it mechanically.

Preserve the recognizable:
- subject
- silhouette
- pose
- spatial relationship
- narrative relationship
- key environmental cues

Use:
- simplified forms
- slightly exaggerated proportions
- symbolic details
- thin imperfect hand-drawn lines
- generous pale negative space
- restrained humorous metaphors when appropriate

Visual references:
- modernist editorial illustration
- Bauhaus composition
- vintage travel illustration
- children's picture books
- fashion sketching
- art publications

## 5. Handmade media

Simulate:
- watercolor
- gouache
- colored pencil
- oil pastel
- dry brush
- pencil or ink linework

Preserve:
- paper grain
- rough edges
- exposed paper
- uneven pigment
- slight misregistration
- imperfect brushwork

Avoid:
- smooth vector graphics
- photorealistic illustration
- 3D rendering
- plastic surfaces
- commercial e-commerce illustration
- excessive cartoon styling

## 6. Color

Derive the palette from the source photograph.

Use:
- limited colors
- high lightness
- low-to-medium saturation
- off-white or pale paper as dominant negative space
- one or two restrained source-derived accents

## 7. Optional graphic details

A small number of the following may be used:
- hand-drawn lines
- dots
- stars
- geometric symbols
- color swatches
- date
- year
- short handwritten phrase

Keep them secondary.

## 8. Quality check

Before output, confirm:

- vertical 3:4
- strict top/bottom 50:50 split
- top remains photographic
- bottom remains hand-drawn rather than vector or 3D
- original scene is instantly recognizable
- illustration palette comes from the original photo
- handmade paper/media texture remains visible
- no commercial template feel
- no unrelated decorative elements

## 9. Output

Use the current agent's image generation or image editing capability to generate the final poster image directly.

Do not output only a prompt or explanation.
