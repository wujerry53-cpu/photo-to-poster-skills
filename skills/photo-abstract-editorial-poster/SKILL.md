# Photo × Abstract Editorial Poster

## Objective

Transform each uploaded photograph into one independent high-end editorial poster.

Never combine multiple uploaded photographs into one collage.
If multiple photos are uploaded, process each image separately.

## 1. Inspect the photo

Identify:

- image orientation
- primary subject
- recognizable silhouette
- major structural relationships
- dominant colors
- lighting
- perspective
- environmental context

## 2. Canvas

Use a vertical 3:4 poster.

If the source photo is landscape:
- divide the main composition horizontally
- photo section = 50%
- abstract section = 50%

If the source photo is portrait:
- divide the main composition vertically
- photo section ≈ 50%
- abstract section ≈ 50%

Use a pale neutral background and generous outer margins.

## 3. Preserve the photograph

Maintain:

- original subject form
- proportions
- perspective
- realistic texture
- natural lighting
- natural shadows
- original color atmosphere
- environmental relationships

Only apply subtle editorial color grading.

If expansion is necessary, extend only non-critical environment such as sky, ground, water, walls, foliage, or distant scenery.

Do not stretch or redesign the subject.

## 4. Build the abstract reinterpretation

Extract the strongest visual structure from the source.

Reinterpret it using:

- clean geometric forms
- flat color blocks
- restrained fine lines
- simplified silhouettes
- structural rhythm
- generous negative space

Do not reproduce every detail.
The graphic should remain recognizable as the same subject.

## 5. Color

Extract a restrained palette from the source photo.

Use:
- dominant source colors
- neutral supporting tones
- one or two restrained accents

Avoid neon colors, unrelated decorative colors, and excessive gradients.

## 6. Typography

Typography is optional.

If used, keep it minimal:
- short English title
- location or theme word
- year
- small subtitle

## 7. Quality check

Before final output, confirm:

- 3:4 vertical canvas
- correct split based on source orientation
- photograph remains faithful to original
- abstract side is geometric rather than realistic
- source palette remains recognizable
- generous negative space
- no template-like commercial styling
- no subject distortion

## 8. Output

Use the current agent's image generation or image editing capability to generate the final poster image directly.

Do not stop at a written description.
