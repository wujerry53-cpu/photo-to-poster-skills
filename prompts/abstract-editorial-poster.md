# Photo × Abstract Editorial Poster Prompt

Upload one photo together with the prompt below.

---

Create a high-end minimalist design poster based on the uploaded photo.

Use an overall 3:4 vertical format.

Determine the source photo orientation before composing:

- For landscape photos, divide the main composition horizontally into equal 50:50 sections.
- For portrait photos, divide the main composition vertically into approximately equal left and right sections.

## Photo Section

Preserve the original photograph as faithfully as possible, including:

- the subject's original form and proportions
- realistic textures
- natural lighting and shadows
- original colors
- perspective
- environmental relationships
- overall atmosphere

Apply only subtle, professional color grading to create the refined look of contemporary editorial and fine-art photography.

If the frame needs to be extended to adapt to the poster layout, naturally expand only the sky, ground, water, walls, foliage, distant scenery, or surrounding environment.

Do not stretch, distort, redesign, beautify, or alter the main subject.

## Graphic Section

Identify the most recognizable silhouette, structure, proportions, rhythm, and visual relationships in the source photo.

Reinterpret them as a simplified abstract graphic composition.

Use:

- clean geometric forms
- flat color blocks
- restrained fine lines
- simplified silhouettes
- strong structural rhythm
- generous negative space

Do not mechanically trace or reproduce every detail.

The result should feel abstract while remaining instantly recognizable as a reinterpretation of the source photograph.

Use a limited color palette extracted from the original photo.

## Background and Composition

Use a light neutral poster background such as beige, ivory, warm white, or pale cream.

Keep:

- generous margins
- calm visual hierarchy
- balanced central composition
- museum-like breathing room

## Typography

Typography is optional.

If appropriate, add only minimal typography such as:

- a short English title
- a location or theme word
- a small subtitle
- a year

Typography should feel refined, understated, and editorial.

## Overall Direction

The final result should reference:

- high-end minimalist editorial design
- contemporary art exhibition posters
- architectural graphic design
- museum-quality art direction
- understated luxury
- refined typography
- generous negative space

Avoid:

- template-like layouts
- low-quality commercial design
- e-commerce aesthetics
- cartoon styles
- realistic illustration in the graphic section
- 3D rendering
- excessive decoration
- gradients as a primary visual device
- neon effects
- overly complex illustration
- arbitrary visual elements
- distortion of the original subject

Generate the final poster directly.
