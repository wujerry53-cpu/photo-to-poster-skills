# Photo × Retro Hand-Drawn Poster Prompt

Upload one photo together with the prompt below.

---

Please turn the uploaded photo into a separate, high-end design poster.

Do not combine multiple photos into a collage; output one poster per photo.

Use an overall 3:4 vertical composition, strictly divided into two equal sections, with the top and bottom each occupying exactly 50% of the canvas height.

## Top Half — Original Photography

Preserve the original photograph, maintaining:

- the subject's structure
- realistic texture
- natural lighting
- natural shadows
- original color atmosphere
- original perspective
- recognizable scene relationships

Apply only subtle, refined photographic color grading to give it the quality of editorial magazine photography and fine-art exhibition imagery.

To adapt the image to the required aspect ratio, naturally extend the sky, ground, water, wall, foliage, or surrounding environment when necessary.

Do not stretch, distort, redesign, beautify, or alter the main subject.

## Bottom Half — Retro Hand-Drawn Editorial Illustration

Extract the most recognizable:

- subject
- silhouette
- pose
- composition
- spatial relationships
- narrative relationships
- signature scene details

Reinterpret them as a light, naïve, retro hand-drawn editorial illustration.

Do not mechanically trace or reproduce every detail.

Instead, reinterpret the subject through:

- simplified forms
- slightly exaggerated proportions
- symbolic features
- humorous visual metaphors where appropriate
- thin, delicate, slightly imperfect hand-drawn lines
- generous pale negative space

Ensure that the original subject and scene remain instantly recognizable.

Blend the aesthetics of:

- modernist editorial illustration
- Bauhaus composition
- children's picture books
- vintage fashion picture books
- travel illustration
- fashion sketching
- sophisticated art publications

The overall feeling should be relaxed, intelligent, playful, and restrained.

## Color

Derive the palette from the photograph above.

Use:

- a limited palette
- high lightness
- low-to-medium saturation
- off-white, ivory, cream, or pale paper as the dominant negative space
- small complementary color accents when useful

Do not introduce unrelated high-saturation colors.

## Mixed-Media Texture

Use a handmade mixed-media character inspired by:

- watercolor
- gouache
- colored pencil
- oil pastel
- dry brush
- pencil or ink linework

Preserve:

- visible paper grain
- rough edges
- areas of exposed paper
- imperfect pigment coverage
- slight misregistration
- imperfect brushwork
- subtle handmade irregularity

Avoid smooth vector graphics, photorealistic illustration, 3D rendering, and plastic-looking surfaces.

## Composition

Keep the overall composition:

- balanced
- airy
- spacious
- restrained
- editorial

A small number of hand-drawn lines, geometric symbols, dots, stars, color swatches, or abstract marks may be added if they support the composition.

Do not decorate randomly.

## Typography

Typography should be used sparingly.

If appropriate, add only:

- a retro handwritten title
- a short phrase
- a date
- a year
- small numbers
- a tiny editorial annotation

Typography should feel naturally integrated into the illustration.

## Overall Direction

Reference:

- vintage fashion picture books
- travel illustration
- art publications
- sophisticated editorial design

The final mood should feel:

- gentle
- nostalgic
- relaxed
- stylish
- humorous
- slightly awkward
- confidently eccentric

Avoid anything that feels:

- overly cartoonish
- cheap
- commercial
- e-commerce-oriented
- glossy
- template-driven
- overly decorative

Generate the final poster directly.
